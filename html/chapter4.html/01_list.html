STEPS TO BAKE A CAKE

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease and flour a cake pan.
  3. In a large bowl, mix together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Add the eggs, milk, and vanilla extract. Beat until smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before frosting or serving.

  • Milk

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking Powder
